Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Font Builder is a free tool that runs in your browser. No account is required.

Your creations

Your drawings, imported graphics, preview text, and font files are processed locally in your browser. Font Builder does not include a server that receives or stores them. The app may save an editable project in your browser's local storage so you can resume work. You can also download a project JSON file and open it again later.

Browser storage is not encrypted by this app. To erase your locally stored draft, clear this site's browser storage. Clearing storage also resets saved preferences.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we load Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand page visits and aggregate actions such as font exports, project saves/opens, and graphic imports. Events may include the number of glyphs, export format, and broad import file type. We do not intentionally send your artwork, preview text, font names, project contents, or filenames to Google Analytics.

Google Analytics may use cookies and process approximate location, page paths, and technical information about browsers and devices. Our configuration disables Google signals and advertising personalization and excludes URL parameters and fragments from the reported page location. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choice

If you decline optional analytics, the GA4 script is not loaded. Your choice and its timestamp are stored locally in your browser, and the tool works the same way whether you consent or not. Use Analytics settings in Font Builder to change your choice. Declining a previously accepted choice disables future analytics collection and reloads the page. Previous analytics records are not automatically removed.

Contact

For privacy questions and data-related requests, use the contact options on Buildiful's official website.
